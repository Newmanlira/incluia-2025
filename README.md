# INCLU.IA 2.0 — Municipal School Support Audit in Brazil

## About

INCLU.IA 2.0 examines the availability of educational support resources in Brazil’s municipal public-school networks. This repository documents an analysis using 2025 School Census data.

The project focuses on Specialized Educational Support (AEE) rooms and special education teaching materials, and examines how reported gaps vary across municipal networks.

## Research focus

- How widely are AEE rooms and special education teaching materials available?
- How do reported resource gaps vary across states and municipalities?
- What patterns emerge when municipal networks are grouped by similar support indicators?

## Data source and scope

The project reports an analysis of more than **128,000 municipal public schools** across **5,569 municipalities**, using the **2025 School Census from INEP/MEC**.

## Methods

The analysis calculates indicators of resource availability and uses **K-means clustering** to explore patterns across municipal networks.

Cluster labels used by INCLU.IA are project-defined analytical categories. They are not official government classifications.

## Findings reported by the project

- **58.04%** of the analyzed schools had no AEE room.
- **58.97%** had no special education teaching materials.
- **2,930 municipalities** were assigned to the project-defined high-vulnerability group.

These figures describe specific indicators from the project’s analysis. They should be interpreted according to the corresponding denominator and inclusion criteria.

## Data outputs

The project’s reported outputs include:

- State-level indicators of resource gaps.
- A municipal ranking of support-resource indicators.
- An analysis of municipal school support.
- K-means cluster assignments for municipal networks.

## Limitations

The indicators describe resources reported in the School Census. They do not, by themselves, establish why a resource is absent, prove policy failure, or demonstrate effects on student learning or teacher wellbeing.

K-means groups municipalities according to similarity across selected variables. The resulting groups are descriptive and should not be interpreted as official classifications or as proof that every municipality in a group has identical conditions.

## Project development

This 2025 phase expands INCLU.IA’s work to municipal-level indicators of educational support. It complements the project’s earlier analysis based on 2019 and 2023 datasets. The two phases use different data periods and analytical scopes.
